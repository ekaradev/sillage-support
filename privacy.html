Sillage Privacy Policy

This policy describes data handling in Sillage: Fragrance Finder, provided by Makbule Amine Bosnalı Kara.

On-device matching

Sillage compares your selected fragrance notes and preferences with its built-in catalog on your device. Sillage works without an account. Your language preference is stored on your device. To remove local app data, delete Sillage in iPhone Settings.

Rewarded advertising

Sillage uses Google AdMob for rewarded videos and Google's User Messaging Platform to display applicable consent choices. Sillage requests non-personalized ads. Google's SDKs may process approximate location, device identifiers, advertising activity, app interactions, crash reports, performance data, and other diagnostics.

Google can use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent abuse, manage consent, and troubleshoot its services. The Google Privacy Policy explains Google's processing, retention, and available privacy controls.

Support and privacy requests

If you email Sillage support, we use the contact information and details you provide to respond and handle follow-up. We keep correspondence as needed to handle your request, maintain a record of its resolution, or meet applicable obligations.

Email aminnebosnali@gmail.com with privacy questions or a request to revoke consent or delete support correspondence. Deleting Sillage removes app data on your device. Data already processed by Google is handled under Google's privacy policy.
